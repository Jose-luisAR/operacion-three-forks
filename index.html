      How to move

      Data used by the app

      Real (NASA)

      • Base image: 25 cm HiRISE of Jezero, layer JEZ_hirise_soc_006 from Mars Trek, with contrast and tint applied. The surroundings use the same layer at 1.3 m/px.
      • 10 tubes: designation, deposit sol, rover position when dropped and tube azimuth — PDS Geosciences, Mars 2020 Sample Depot.
      • Tube photos: Perseverance Front Hazcam images taken right after each tube was dropped, from Mars 2020 Raw Images (converted to grayscale).
      • Rover traverse: M20_waypoints.json (MMGIS).
      • Weather: MEDA station, Mars 2020 API.
      • Final panorama: Mastcam-Z, sol 693, from the PDS depot bundle.

      Assumptions (mission design)

      These values do not come from NASA data because no human has walked on Mars. They can be changed in the “Mission assumptions” panel.
